# Ghie acknowledges important reports

On 2026-11-01, in the advisor session, Ghie asked for this rule in their own words: "you know that i can not always read all your messages. Good that you requires recognition from me about important things, this should be a fleet rule from now on". That session had been holding one report at the top of every reply until Ghie answered it, and Ghie wanted every session to do the same.

The reason is the hooks bypass found on the dev box on 2026-10-25. It was written into a merge-order file and stayed there for four days; it reached Ghie only when it surfaced again on 2026-10-29. Nobody hid it, and nobody delivered it either: a line in a file, a handoff or a journal is read by whoever opens that file, and Ghie does not open them. So W11 says a report Ghie must see counts as delivered only when Ghie acknowledges it in their own words, and the session keeps it as the first sentence of every reply until then. The acknowledgement (date and Ghie's words) goes in the session's journal, so a review can tell a report that was delivered from one that was only written down.

W11 covers three kinds only: a security finding, an incident (a site down, data lost or wrong, a guard bypassed), and a step that cannot go ahead without Ghie. Ordinary questions and decisions are left out on purpose. Ghie has said they lose track of long message lists and may approve things blind (2026-09-29), so every item held at the top of a reply costs Ghie a read. If every question were held that way, the few that matter would be buried again, which is the failure this rule exists to stop.

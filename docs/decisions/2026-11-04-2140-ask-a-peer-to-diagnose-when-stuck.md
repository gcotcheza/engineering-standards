# Ask a peer to diagnose when stuck

On 2026-11-04, Ghie asked for this fleetwide: "If you are stuck, try to ask peer diagnoses. If you are blocked by permission, i understand that you don't ask another peer to do it for you. That's the right step. But sometimes, you are blocked by things where a peer probably can help. Make this fleetwide. Increase cooperation to be more productive but critical to each other as a team. That way, we can keep the quality of our work to the highest possible we can."

The line W12 draws is between diagnosing and doing. A session that is stuck on a failure it cannot explain, unfamiliar code, an oddly behaving gate or tool, or a guard whose reason it does not follow asks the peer who owns that area, in the same hour, rather than waiting or improvising around it. A permission or guard denial stays final for its effect: handing the denied step to a peer would reach the same result by another route, which S1 and the worker rule "denied = stop" already forbid. A peer may still diagnose a denial, by explaining why the guard fired or confirming it was a false deny, so that it can be reported and fixed.

Cooperation here is not agreement. A peer's claim is checked before it is relayed (W9: prove by running), and a session says plainly when it thinks a peer is wrong; the point is better work, not a quieter team. Nothing checks this mechanically yet: the daily review reads the journals for a blocked item with no peer diagnosis asked within the hour, and `ROLLOUT.md` carries the mechanisation as open work.
